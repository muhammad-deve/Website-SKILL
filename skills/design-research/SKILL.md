# Design research

Use [methodology](../../research/methodology.md) and the
[source catalog](../../research/sources.json). Search existing records first.
For a repository, inspect README, license, entrypoint and relevant executable or
data files; record revision and scope. A large star count is not evidence of quality.

For a reference website, record the current URL and inspection date. Observe desktop
and mobile when tooling permits. Describe a hierarchy, type role, composition and
interaction precisely; separate computed values from visual inference. Trace an
award to its organization and exact designation. Current live design may differ
from its award-year version. Do not equate a gallery entry with winning an award.

Use [reference template](../../templates/reference-analysis.md). Transfer a principle
and implementation hypothesis, not branding, copy or a proprietary layout. Keep
unlicensed screenshots local. Verify licensing before incorporating files and add
a record to [provenance](../../research/provenance.json).

For Figma or screenshot input, distinguish inspectable design tokens from inferred
measurements. Use available authorized tools; document unavailable integrations.
An image cannot reveal all interaction states, semantics or responsive rules.
Treat community claims as opinions until a reproducible comparison supports them.
