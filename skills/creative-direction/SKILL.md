# Creative direction

Read the real content before choosing a look. Name the audience's decision and the
brand's most tangible evidence: a material, workflow, place, craft or point of view.
An existing brand system is a constraint unless the user requested replacement.

For an open brief, compare two or three genuinely different directions in compact
notes: what dominates, what stays quiet, why it fits, what assets it needs. Select a
direction and proceed when the choice is reversible. Don't make the user approve
routine styling. Explicitly requested directions govern over personal preferences.

Use the axes in [taxonomy](../../knowledge/taxonomy.json), then retrieve a deep style
profile. A product category such as fintech is not a mandate for navy, gradients or
a dashboard. Treat unmatched aesthetics as research work.
Keep one primary composition logic and one optional supporting influence. Record
their roles using [combination rules](../../knowledge/styles/combining.md).

Specify type by role and metrics, color by semantic pairs, spacing by relationships
and imagery by subject/crop. Translate adjectives into observable decisions.
Fill the [brief](../../templates/design-brief.md) only to the depth needed to maintain
consistency. Three adjectives and a moodboard do not replace content hierarchy.
Do not invent claims, customers, prices or performance figures as brand proof.
