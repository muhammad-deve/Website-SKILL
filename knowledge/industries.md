# Industry decisions: initial coverage

These are original planning heuristics. They are context-sensitive and are not
claims about conversion, regulation or user research in every industry.

| Context | Audience decision and trust | Useful patterns | Failure to avoid | Safe room for expression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS / AI tools | Can this solve my task, and what does it actually do? | Real workflow preview, limits, clear trial state | Invented productivity figures or generic sparkles | Campaign art can vary while app controls stay consistent |
| Fintech / enterprise | What changed, what needs attention, can I recover? | Units, comparison periods, exceptions, audit context | Status by color alone; unexplained charts | Precise typography and bespoke data hierarchy |
| Developer tools / security | How does it integrate and what are its boundaries? | Real commands, architecture, inspectable docs | Fake terminal output or unsupported security claims | Technical diagrams and restrained industrial cues |
| Healthcare / public services | Can I understand and complete this under stress? | Plain labels, error recovery, privacy context | Low contrast, novelty navigation, ambiguous next steps | Brand voice and supportive illustration outside critical controls |
| Education | Where am I and what should I learn next? | Progress, worked examples, readable lessons | Progress decoration without meaningful feedback | Playful illustration with a stable learning structure |
| Commerce / luxury / fashion | Is it right for me and what will I pay? | Material details, variants, sizing, delivery and returns | Hidden price or editorial navigation blocking purchase | Strong art direction with clear transaction controls |
| Hospitality / travel / real estate | Where is it, what is available and what is included? | Location, dates, availability, factual imagery | Beautiful but unusable booking flows | Photography and narrative pacing |
| Entertainment / gaming / media | What is this experience and how do I join? | Program, access, preview, time and place | Autoplay overload and inaccessible schedule | Maximalism, genre-specific typography and expressive motion |
| Portfolios / agencies | What work was done and by whom? | Case studies, role, process and outcomes with evidence | Unattributed work or decorative project statistics | Unusual composition around intelligible project navigation |
| Nonprofits / government | Is this credible, who benefits, how do I act? | Transparent purpose, accessible forms, service eligibility | Guilt-driven clutter or inaccessible donation controls | Human imagery and clear storytelling |
| Consumer apps | Can I complete a frequent task easily? | Familiar navigation, clear state, recoverable actions | Novel controls that slow repetition | Personality in feedback and content, not unpredictable behavior |

Rendered reference: [GOV.UK](../references/catalog.json). Text/DOM examples for
product communication: Stripe, Linear and Figma; their evidence limitations are in
the same catalog. Deep per-industry research, startup selection, and broader reference
coverage remain in the continuation plan. Do not use these rows as universal rules.
