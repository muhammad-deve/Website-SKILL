Concept work / 2026

Community / adaptive reuse

The open hall

A former workshop imagined as a shared room for the neighborhood. One generous table becomes the center of a flexible public space.

Read the design approach

Retain the existing frame. Introduce north-facing light and a continuous accessible route. Movable storage lets the room shift between making, meeting and eating.

Landscape / small structures

A place to pause

A shaded pavilion at the edge of a walking route. The structure frames a view while leaving room for people to make it their own.

Read the design approach

A repeated timber frame provides shade without enclosing the site. Seating sits alongside a level path so that pausing does not interrupt movement.
